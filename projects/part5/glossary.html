Glossary of Stats Terms

New to advanced stats? Start here.

🔍 Search terms...

QBR

Total Quarterback Rating — a 0–100 score of a quarterback's overall contribution on every play.

Passer Rating

A formula combining completion %, yards, touchdowns, and interceptions per attempt.

YAC

Yards After Catch — yards a receiver gains after securing the ball.

DVOA

Defense-adjusted Value Over Average — team efficiency compared to league average, adjusted for opponent.

EPA

Expected Points Added — how much a single play changed a team's expected points.

Snap Count

The number of plays a player was on the field for.

Red Zone Efficiency

Percentage of trips inside the opponent's 20-yard line that end in a touchdown.

Third Down %

How often a team converts third downs into a new set of downs.

Turnover Margin

Takeaways minus giveaways over a game or season.

Yards Per Attempt

Passing yards divided by pass attempts.
